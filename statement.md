# Problem Statement

Most first year college students have some skill they are good at (like Guitar, Sketching, Excel, Public Speaking) but they also want to learn a
completely different skill from someone else. Paid classes/tutorials are often expensive, and it is hard to find someone nearby who is willing to
teach you for free.

## Scope of the Project
SkillSwap solves this by letting students list what they can teach and what they want to learn, and then matches them with other students who have complementary skills. Students can then send exchange requests, schedule sessions, and rate each other after the session is done.

The scope of this version is limited to a single machine, terminal based application that stores all data in local `.txt` files. It does not include real-time chat, payments, or a web interface.

## Target Users
- College students who want to learn a new skill for free
- College students who want to share/teach a skill they already know

## High-Level Features
- Registration / Login
- Profile management (teaching and learning skill lists)
- Skill based matching between students
- Exchange request system (send, accept, reject)
- Session scheduling
- Feedback and rating system after a session
